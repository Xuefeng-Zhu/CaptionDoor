## Product outcome

The primary journey is now: visible bell alert → explicit responsibility claim → explicit incoming caption start → understand incoming words → handle in person → record closure. The app cannot perform the in-person action and does not send audio to the Ring speaker. Visitor text, explicit thread association and approved web replies remain behind **Visitor text (optional)**, with their original separate acknowledgement stages.

- Home gives the current alert the first, larger card and shorter heading. Empty state says **No open visit** rather than implying no person is present. Connection/offline status remains explicit. Non-demo readiness directs users to check Ring access rather than implying it was verified.
- Onboarding describes incoming speech and handling in person. Separate media, AWS transcription and sharing consent, access-retention/deletion distinction, invitations and notifications remain. The visitor sign is optional.
- Caption actions are above the transcript. Demo start is offered only before a session, not as a cure for unavailable/ended media. Demo provenance, Updating… and Finalized stay separate from verified truth. Ended media gives an explicit in-person/closure next action while responsibility and permitted history remain.
- Removed repeated mobile statuses and fixed an existing CSS override that forced 18px caption text despite selecting 36px. Caption text now honors 28/32/36px on mobile. Focus outlines, reduced motion, scrolled-position/Return to live behavior remain.
- Incoming video remains collapsed. Audio selection and capture are explicit, remote-only and separate from camera/caption permissions. Revoking caption access hides private words and disables capture while independently authorized video controls remain usable. Earlier visit history is beside captions.
